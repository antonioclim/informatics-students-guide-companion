# Book and companion roles

The book contains the stable reasoning, definitions, decision boundaries and gate criteria required for independent use. The companion provides editable records, extended specimens, dated source registers, synthetic worked examples and technical checks.

Moving operational appendix material to the Digital Companion does not remove indispensable methodological principles from the book. In book v3.9.0, those principles are integrated into the main text and editable operational resources are maintained through DC-A–DC-E.

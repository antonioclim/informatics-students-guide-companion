# MEASURE — Quantitative empirical study

**Locked route meaning:** What distribution, association or effect is supported by observed data?  
**Minimum planning evidence:** Constructs, sampling, protocol, uncertainty and validity threats.  
**Immediate warning:** Association or before–after change becomes causation.  
**Claim boundary:** Threshold crossing becomes importance, causality or generalisability

## Hostile defence lens

**Question:** Why does the result not establish a causal educational effect?  
**Bounded answer:** The claim is limited to the declared design and synthetic worked data. The task, sample and missingness boundaries prevent broad causal or long-term learning conclusions.

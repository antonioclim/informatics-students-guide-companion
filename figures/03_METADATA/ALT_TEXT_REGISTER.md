# Alt-text registry

Source: drawing descriptions embedded in the v3.1 master.

## Figure 1.1 — The evidence-to-defence architecture: eight inquiry routes, four controls and nine gates

A three-row serpentine architecture. Eight inquiry routes enter a nine-gate chain: Project Start Pack, Problem, Evidence, Design, Execution, Claim, Argument, Editorial and Defence Readiness. SCOPE, CORPUS and DECIDE are local controls at the Problem, Evidence and Design gates. TRACE spans the chain. A dashed return path shows that new evidence may reopen an earlier gate.

## Figure 1.2 — Advice, approval, authority and assistance do not transfer authorship or responsibility

A central student box owns authorship, execution, verification, decision records and defence. Arrows enter from the supervisor for advice and academic approval, institutional authority for rules and assessment, an external organisation for context and access and an AI tool for candidate assistance. None transfers responsibility for the submitted record.

## Figure 2.1 — From candidate ideas to the Problem Gate

A six-step decision path moves from candidate ideas through topic screening, SCOPE framing and a critical-assumption test to a recorded decision and the Problem Gate. The decision must be continue, narrow, pivot or stop. A dashed feedback path returns narrowed or pivoted work to SCOPE; only supported continuation reaches the gate.

## Figure 2.2 — The SCOPE framing canvas

The SCOPE canvas surrounds a tractable project with five interdependent fields: Situation and stakeholders, Change sought and constraints, Outcomes and objectives, Planned evidence and performance criteria, and Exclusions, assumptions and ethical boundaries. Every field records evidence and an unresolved uncertainty.

## Figure 3.1 — From discovery record to claim-linked evidence and project decision

A seven-stage chain moves from a documented search route to a discovery record, verified source object, identity resolution, reading and appraisal, claim-linked evidence and a project decision. Identity resolution maps related publications to one study or versions to an authoritative object. A dashed feedback path shows that evidence may reopen the search or Problem Gate.

## Figure 3.2 — The CORPUS cycle and its retained evidence records

The CORPUS cycle contains six stages around an Evidence Base Pack. Collect produces a Search Strategy Record; Organise produces a Source Ledger and identity map; Read produces a Core Source Note; Probe produces an Appraisal Record; Use produces a Claim–Evidence Map; Synthesise produces a Synthesis Memo and Corpus Statement. Arrows show the iterative cycle and dashed links connect each retained record to the central pack.

## Figure 4.1 — The reversible driver-to-test chain

A reversible chain moves from evidence and authority through driver and boundary, criteria, credible alternatives and trade-off to a decision with a reconsideration trigger, a protocol or artefact, an early test and an evaluation result. A dashed feedback path shows that failed evidence or changed authority reopens the upstream commitment.

## Figure 4.2 — DECIDE as a cycle of conditional commitment

Six DECIDE operations surround a conditional commitment. Define records drivers, boundaries and authority; Establish links evidence and criteria; Construct creates credible alternatives; Inspect examines implications, threats and reversibility; Decide records rationale, owner and trigger; Evaluate Early probes the highest-risk assumption. Arrows show the iterative cycle and dashed links connect every operation to the conditional commitment.

## Figure 5.1 — Candidate-to-admission chain for one consequential work unit

A top-row chain moves from approved commitment through bounded work unit, frozen starting state, candidate output and inspection with local checks. A disposition box sends accepted work to an accepted work unit, Execution Dossier and Chapter 6 evaluation. A dashed return path shows that repair, repetition, missing authority or new evidence can reopen preparation or an earlier gate. The figure states that acceptance does not prove the thesis claim.

## Figure 5.2 — The bounded execution loop and its stop points

Six stages surround one consequential work unit: authorise and bound, freeze state, produce candidate work, inspect the primary artefact and checks, decide disposition, then record and recover. A stop notice precedes external or irreversible action. The lower note states that failed runs and rejected candidates remain in the record.

## Figure 6.1 — The compressed evidence-to-claim ladder and forbidden shortcuts

Nine numbered boxes form a compressed, snake-shaped evidence-to-claim ladder: executed output, observation, metric or estimate, comparison or inference, evidence, interpretation, claim boundary, bounded claim and contribution. A red panel prohibits direct jumps from output to claim and from metric to contribution. The figure states that conditions, uncertainty, alternatives and excluded scope govern every transition.

## Figure 6.2 — The claim stress-test and gate return paths

Six operations surround the minimum meaningful claim: bound wording, match route and comparison, characterise uncertainty or credibility, challenge assumptions and counter-evidence, reproduce or audit, then decide the strongest supported boundary. The cycle can repair evaluation, return to execution, reopen design, evidence or problem, or narrow or suspend the claim.

## Figure 7.1 — Gate records, answer architecture and manuscript section functions

Three horizontal layers distinguish authorised gate records, a seven-step answer architecture and manuscript section functions. Problem, Evidence, Design, Execution and Claim records feed a central spine running from question through position, route, evidence, result and boundary to contribution. The lower layer shows introduction, knowledge position, method or design, results or findings and discussion or conclusion. A note states that records are sources rather than pre-written chapters and that every reuse must preserve identity and boundary.

## Figure 7.2 — Section contract and paragraph reasoning test

A five-step section contract runs from promise through evidence set, reasoning and boundary to return. A separate four-step paragraph test runs from claim through evidence and reasoning to a limit or hand-off. Two notes state that a section earns its place only when the next step depends on what it establishes and that a citation or result beside a claim is insufficient without a visible reasoning link and boundary.

## Figure 8.1 — Semantic-first revision and proof-control pipeline

A semantic-first editorial pipeline moves from the accepted study through macro, meso and micro revision, package control and Microsoft Word proof. Semantic drift in evidence, comparison, uncertainty, population, causal status or claim scope returns to the earliest responsible gate. Technical defects trigger a cycle of DOCX repair, all-story field update, tagged PDF export, every-page inspection and proof recording. The final status distinguishes author-side closed with proof pending from author-side proof verified with administrative and publisher checks still pending.

## Figure 8.2 — One-study consistency across headline, authorship and manuscript layers

An authoritative study containing the question, executed route, evidence, principal result and boundary supplies title, verified author identity, keywords, abstract, introduction, results, discussion, limitations, conclusion and version metadata. An equivalence test requires every interface to describe the same study and bounded answer. Compression may omit detail but may not change identity, count, uncertainty or claim scope; detected drift is repaired at the earliest layer where it entered.

## Figure 9.1 — From author-side closure to a defence-ready institutional record

A five-stage chain moves from author-side closure through a current authority map, an assessable institutional record and controlled performance interfaces to defence readiness. One bounded contribution must preserve one title, route, evidence body, claim boundary and assistance record. Scientific identity drift, inaccurate declarations, unsafe evidence or unresolved authority conflicts require a return to the earliest responsible control. Final checks confirm current rules, identified files, accurate declarations, lawful evidence and tested recovery.

## Figure 9.2 — One bounded contribution across institutional, evidence and defence interfaces

One bounded contribution is surrounded by four controlled interfaces: the institutional record, private evidence package, demonstration and slides and oral answers. Every interface preserves the same question, evidence, judgement, uncertainty, limitation and assistance record. A non-compensation rule states that strong performance cannot repair an inaccurate record, unsafe package or unsupported claim.

# Zenodo deposit hold

No Zenodo record has been created or confirmed for this project. No DOI is asserted.

Do not reserve or publish a DOI until:

- the deposit object and exclusions are fixed;
- creator metadata are verified;
- the licence decision is explicit;
- publisher and institutional rights are clear;
- personal and confidential content has been excluded;
- the GitHub release state is known;
- the final repository manifest and hashes are stable.

If a DOI is later reserved in a draft, it must not be described as a published DOI until the record is published.

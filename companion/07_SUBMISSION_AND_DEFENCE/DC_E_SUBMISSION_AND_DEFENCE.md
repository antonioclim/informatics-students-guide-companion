The final evidence-backed control before submission and oral examination

A checklist is useful only when a tick denotes an observed state. Do not tick an item because it is planned, probably correct or visible in an unverified interface. Tick it only after the required object, receipt, decision, test result or written clarification exists and can be retrieved. These checklists convert the Defence Readiness Gate in Chapter 9 into a compact operating record. They do not replace the route-specific regulations, current-session calendar, official forms or instructions summarised in Digital Companion modules DC-A–DC-D.

The source set was checked on 23 August 2026. Current official instructions prevail over this dated aid. The bachelor’s and master’s routes remain distinct: do not import the number of tests, speaking time, theoretical component, dossier items or submission channel from one route into the other. Where official sources conflict, mark CONFLICT, pause the affected action and obtain dated written clarification from the competent authority. Silence, habit and a prior cohort’s procedure are not clarification.

The two August 2026 mobility-only announcements discovered during the Phase 14C check are conditional exceptions. Their target pages and attachments remain unverified in this package. Do not tick a mobility-route item or infer a deadline until the current page, attachments, candidate-status evidence and competent confirmation have been retained.

Class M identifies a current mandatory official requirement or a non-negotiable integrity condition. Class C identifies an official requirement activated by route, status, cohort, mobility, fee, identity, account access, demonstration condition or another stated trigger. Once activated, a Class C item is mandatory. Class R identifies an authorial control that reduces ambiguity, version drift or operational failure without claiming independent institutional force. Record N/A only with the reason and authority for non-applicability. PENDING, CONFLICT and BLOCKED cannot be converted into a tick.

## How to use the checklists

Create one dated working copy for the actual examination route. Enter the current authority set and session before using any table. Preserve the completed copy in the restricted Defence Readiness Dossier with the evidence it cites. A tick must remain intelligible to an authorised second person who did not assemble the package. Where an item fails, record the defect, earliest responsible gate, repair owner, due date and retest evidence. Do not erase a failed state after repair; close it through a dated disposition.

Use this evidence hierarchy: official decision or confirmation, controlled file or record, independent test result, dated receipt, then explanatory note. A filename, local existence, screenshot without context or verbal assurance is not equivalent to confirmation. In particular, transmitted does not mean enrolled, uploaded does not mean accepted and opened once does not mean recoverable under examination conditions.

## Submit the dossier and prove enrolment

Submission has at least three distinguishable states: possession of a local file, successful transmission and confirmed administrative enrolment. The first does not prove the second and the second does not prove the third. Current bachelor’s and master’s regulations require the candidate to ensure that the final file is complete and openable and to check the confirmation issued after document verification and enrolment. An incomplete dossier is not cured by a successful file upload.

## Demonstration and fallback

A demonstration is route- and project-dependent. It may be required, permitted, discouraged or unnecessary. Confirm the applicable condition before preparing one. A fallback supports recovery but does not silently replace a live demonstration when the current rules explicitly require live operation. Every performance asset must represent the same release, data state and claim as the frozen record.

## Presentation and accessibility

The presentation is a retrieval interface for the assessed record, not a second manuscript. Build it around the problem, decisive contribution, supporting evidence, boundary that prevents overclaiming and defensible implication. Current route-specific conditions control duration, test structure, room, equipment and permitted aids.

## Oral ownership and hostile questions

The oral examination tests whether the student can retrieve, explain and bound the record under questioning. Memorised fluency is insufficient. A defensible answer normally contains a direct answer, decisive evidence, principal boundary and implication or repair. An honest unknown and a bounded correction are stronger than an invented detail.

## Defence day, incident response and closeout

Defence day should contain no unresolved scientific choice. It is an execution and retrieval event under the current official conditions. Closeout begins immediately afterwards: retain the examined state, official communications and authorised corrections while preserving the separation between restricted evidence and any later public release.

## Final readiness decision

Record PASS only when every Class M item and activated Class C item is ticked, no required value remains PENDING or CONFLICT and every Class R exception is named, justified and accepted by the responsible person. A page of ticks is insufficient if the cited evidence cannot be retrieved or if the check used a different scientific state.

Block submission or return to the responsible gate when the dossier is incomplete, enrolment is unconfirmed, official instructions remain unresolved, the examination copy differs scientifically from the validated work, a mandatory declaration is absent or inaccurate, protected information is exposed, a required demonstration is unsafe or unavailable, hidden generative answer support is planned or the student cannot explain the principal claim and decisive evidence.

A local formatting defect may be repaired locally. A scientific discrepancy cannot. Return an altered dataset or corpus, changed result, unsupported conclusion or non-reproducible method to the earliest gate that could have prevented it, then repeat every downstream check affected by the repair. Defence readiness authorises presentation of one verified record under current rules. It does not predict the mark and does not authorise public release.

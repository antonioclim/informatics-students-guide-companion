A route-specific sequence with a current-session authority record

An administratively admissible thesis or dissertation is not produced by following a remembered sequence. It is produced by identifying the applicable route, fixing the authority set for the current session and retaining evidence that each consequential gate was passed. This module provides that control structure for the bachelor’s and master’s routes of the Bucharest University of Economic Studies (ASE), with faculty-level operational checks for the Faculty of Cybernetics, Statistics and Economic Informatics (CSIE).

The source set was checked on 23 August 2026. Current official regulations, calendars, faculty notices, forms and valid written instructions prevail over this dated module. Exact dates, hours, fees, rooms, platform labels, file names, form numbers and cohort labels are intentionally not reproduced as permanent rules. They belong in the current-session record and must be verified again before every consequential action.

Mandatory identifies a requirement supported directly by the applicable official route. Conditional identifies an official requirement triggered only by candidate status or circumstance. Recommended identifies an authorial control that strengthens traceability but does not claim independent institutional force. A live page is not treated as internally consistent merely because it is official: unresolved discrepancies are recorded as conflicts and stop the affected action until authoritative clarification is obtained.

## Conflict and supersession rules

First, compare like with like. Confirm route, faculty, programme, candidate status and session before treating two instructions as competing. A previous-cohort requirement may be conditional rather than contradictory.

Second, apply the authority hierarchy. The regulation in force governs. A current institutional calendar and current faculty or programme instruction operationalise that regulation. A current official form and a valid written coordinator instruction are usable only within the governing rule. This book applies only where those sources do not settle the question.

Third, never average conflicting dates or hours, select the more convenient value or treat publication recency as proof of correctness. Mark the row CONFLICT, pause the affected action and obtain written clarification from the competent official source. Record the resolution, author, date and retained evidence.

Fourth, repeat the check immediately before every consequential event. At the verification date, live CSIE pages and their linked 2026 notices disagreed on same-day registration cut-off times and retained inconsistent cohort or session labels. The discrepancy is evidence that an official web location can still contain unresolved version-control defects. Neither conflicting value is reproduced here as a permanent instruction.

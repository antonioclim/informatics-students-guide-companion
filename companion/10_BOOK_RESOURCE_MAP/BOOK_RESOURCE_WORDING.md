# Book-facing companion wording

The final book identifies the stable repository at https://github.com/antonioclim/informatics-students-guide-companion. It describes the Digital Companion as a versioned collection of editable workbook records, templates, route kits, synthetic examples, schemas, registers and figure sources. Versioned archival copies are preserved through Zenodo. Readers should consult verified release metadata for the DOI assigned to the version used; the book itself does not depend on that DOI.

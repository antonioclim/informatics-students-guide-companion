# Maintenance and update policy

Digital Companion v1.2.0 is a versioned support object. Durable methodological controls change only through an explicit versioned revision. Institutional links, calendars, portal instructions and other volatile values must be reverified before consequential use and should record source, access date and disposition.

Any accepted change must update the affected object, crosswalks, manifests, hashes and release notes. Figure changes must be reconciled across the ODG authority and SVG, PPTX and PNG derivatives. No manuscript, proof, personal data or publisher-facing file may be introduced through a maintenance change.

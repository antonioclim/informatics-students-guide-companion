What must exist, who checks it, where it is retained and what its absence reopens

A project is not controlled merely because its files exist. A defensible record must connect each consequential decision to the object reviewed, the person or authority competent to accept it, the version that was acted upon and the evidence retained afterwards. This module converts the book’s gate architecture into a milestone inventory and then separates that scientific record from the official closeout dossier required for bachelor’s and master’s routes at the Bucharest University of Economic Studies (ASE).

Three record systems must remain distinct. The scientific project record supports reasoning, execution and claims. The administrative dossier supports eligibility, transmission and registration. The release record supports publication, deposit or examination delivery. A receipt in one system cannot repair a missing record in another: a successful upload does not validate a claim, an excellent analysis does not replace a required declaration and a public repository is not an appropriate store for identity documents, signatures or payment evidence.

Mandatory identifies an official obligation for the stated route or a minimum record without which the book’s gate cannot be defended. Conditional identifies an official or scientific record activated by status, method, data, risk or circumstance. Recommended identifies an authorial control that improves traceability without claiming independent institutional force. The book’s gates and dossier names are authorial decision controls, not official ASE categories.

## Retention architecture and release boundary

Use four controlled zones. The authoritative project workspace contains the scientific record, one controlled manuscript line and links to evidence. The restricted research area contains personal, confidential, licensed or security-sensitive material. The administrative dossier contains forms, identity records, signatures, fee evidence and official confirmations. The release area contains only objects cleared for examination, publisher delivery or public deposit.

Do not place administrative records, participant identities, consent forms, confidential raw data, access credentials, unredacted similarity reports, signatures or payment evidence in GitHub, Zenodo or a public companion. A manifest may record that a restricted object exists without reproducing its contents. Access, retention and deletion must follow the current controller, institutional and legal requirements rather than a generic period invented by this guide.

For every consequential object, retain a minimum control tuple: descriptive identifier, route or gate, owner, reviewer or authority, version or date, location, status, checksum where useful and disposition. Where the object is superseded, preserve the decision and relationship to the new version. Do not overwrite the evidence that explains why a later version exists.

Closeout is complete only when the scientific record, administrative confirmation and release package agree on the exact title, author identity, final file and declared scope. If they do not, the discrepancy is classified before submission. Administrative correction remains administrative only if it leaves the accepted study unchanged; any scientific change reopens the responsible gate and requires a new proof.

# Changelog

## 1.1.1 - 2026-08-19

- Migrated the active title from **The Informatics Student’s Guide** to **The Informatics Student’s Guide to Research Projects, Theses and Dissertations**.
- Preserved the subtitle *From Topic Selection to Evidence, Implementation, Writing and Defence*.
- Added both authors to active metadata and workbook materials.
- Replaced “authorial edition” with “authorial master”.
- Split the record architecture into 12 minimum and 33 full CSV templates.
- Added a route selector, feasibility record, search and appraisal records, execution and evaluation records, editorial closure records and defence-readiness records.
- Added four synthetic worked route kits for algorithmic/simulation, qualitative inquiry, case study and hybrid route.
- Added dated institutional and tool capability registries.
- Added maintenance, supersession, release and link-check controls.
- Aligned workbook, route examples, registries, manifests and source-master references with master v3.1.
- Excluded archived institutional HTML and v1.0.0 provenance from the repository candidate while preserving them in the private handover.
- Maintained the no-public-release, no-DOI and no-public-licence hold.

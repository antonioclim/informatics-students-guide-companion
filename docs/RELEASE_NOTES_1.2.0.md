# Student’s Guide Digital Companion v1.2.0

This release aligns the Digital Companion with *Student’s Guide to Research Projects, Theses and Dissertations: From Topic Selection to Evidence, Implementation, Writing and Defence*, book v3.9.0 and Figure Authority v4.3.

## Included

- companion workbook, quick-start guidance and minimum and full templates;
- eight route kits and privacy-safe synthetic worked examples;
- DC-A–DC-E operational modules, including the complete submission and defence module;
- institutional-source, change, supersession and book-resource registers;
- 18 figure sets in SVG, PNG, ODG and PPTX formats;
- schemas, validation scripts, GitHub Actions workflow, manifests and SHA-256 registers.

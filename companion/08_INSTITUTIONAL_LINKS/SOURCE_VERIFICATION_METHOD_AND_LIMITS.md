# Current-source verification method and limits

**Controlled check date:** 23 August 2026  
**Scope:** source identity, supersession indicators, route labels, current-session volatility, cross-source disagreement and conditional exception discovery for DC-A–DC-E.  
**Status:** private control evidence; not an institutional interpretation, legal opinion or guarantee of future source availability.

The controlled check used direct official pages and PDFs, official decision indexes, DOI or publisher identity records and comparison between live faculty pages and their linked session notices. Identity verification establishes that the cited object or official location was found. It does not establish that every current operational value is complete, internally consistent or still applicable when a student acts.

The check detected four controlled conflicts. The bachelor’s live page and its linked July 2026 PDF state different same-day registration cut-offs. The dissertation live page and its linked June 2026 PDF also state different cut-offs. In addition, the live pages retain stale cohort or session labels. None of the conflicting values is selected or reproduced as a permanent instruction. See [`CURRENT_SOURCE_CONFLICT_REGISTER_2026-08-23.csv`](CURRENT_SOURCE_CONFLICT_REGISTER_2026-08-23.csv).

The official CSIE index also exposes two August 2026 announcements restricted to candidates participating in international mobility programmes. The target pages and attachments were not retrieved during the controlled check. The package therefore records only the existence and restricted scope of the announcements; it freezes no deadline, dossier or eligibility value. See [`CURRENT_SESSION_EXCEPTION_REGISTER_2026-08-23.csv`](CURRENT_SESSION_EXCEPTION_REGISTER_2026-08-23.csv).

Before consequential action, the user must confirm the route, candidate status, session, source revision and any narrower competent written instruction. A `CONFLICT`, `PENDING OFFICIAL RELEASE`, `PAYLOAD UNVERIFIED` or stale-label finding blocks use of the affected value. Previous-session notices and calendars remain provenance, not templates for a later session.

The complete verification record is [`CURRENT_SOURCE_VERIFICATION_LOG_2026-08-23.csv`](CURRENT_SOURCE_VERIFICATION_LOG_2026-08-23.csv). The source concordance supplies canonical identities, while the volatile-source register controls recheck obligations. Neither file replaces the source itself.

# SYNTHESISE — Formal evidence synthesis

**Locked route meaning:** What does a documented corpus support?  
**Minimum planning evidence:** Protocol, reproducible search, study identity, appraisal and synthesis.  
**Immediate warning:** Paper summaries or search failure become consensus or novelty.  
**Claim boundary:** Number of favourable publications becomes consensus or universal effectiveness

Use `ROUTE_GATE_PLAN.csv` to see what evidence the common nine-gate chain requires for this route. Use `ROUTE_RECORD_SELECTION.csv` to select shared templates and `RISK_AND_BOUNDARY_REGISTER.csv` to preserve the principal failure signatures. The `worked_example` folder is synthetic and schema-aligned. It is a teaching object, not an empirical result or a substitute for an approved project protocol.

## Demonstration lens

Trace one finding from search record to publication cluster, underlying study, appraisal, synthesis memo and bounded claim.

## Hostile defence lens

**Question:** How do you know the review did not double-count one study?  
**Bounded answer:** The corpus preserves publication, record and study identities separately and the claim is based on the resolved study set rather than citation count.

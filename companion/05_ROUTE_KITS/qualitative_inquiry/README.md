# INTERPRET — Qualitative inquiry

**Locked route meaning:** How do participants experience or make sense of a phenomenon?  
**Minimum planning evidence:** Sampling rationale, protocol, reflexive analysis and traceable interpretation.  
**Immediate warning:** Themes are treated as population frequencies.  
**Claim boundary:** Recurring theme becomes population prevalence or universal truth

Use `ROUTE_GATE_PLAN.csv` to see what evidence the common nine-gate chain requires for this route. Use `ROUTE_RECORD_SELECTION.csv` to select shared templates and `RISK_AND_BOUNDARY_REGISTER.csv` to preserve the principal failure signatures. The `worked_example` folder is synthetic and schema-aligned. It is a teaching object, not an empirical result or a substitute for an approved project protocol.

## Demonstration lens

Trace one theme from synthetic extract to code, memo, negative case and bounded interpretation without presenting the quotation as real data.

## Hostile defence lens

**Question:** How can an interpretive result be trusted without statistical reliability?  
**Bounded answer:** The route does not claim statistical estimation. Trustworthiness depends on transparent sampling, analytic trace, reflexivity, negative cases and a bounded interpretation.

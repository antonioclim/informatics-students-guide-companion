A dated control aid for degree-completion work

This module translates the current finalisation rules of the Bucharest University of Economic Studies (ASE) and the current operational pages of the Faculty of Cybernetics, Statistics and Economic Informatics (CSIE) into a compact control aid. The official sources were checked on 23 August 2026. Current regulations, programme instructions, forms, calendars and valid written decisions prevail over this module whenever they have been amended or specify a narrower route. Students at another institution must replace the ASE/CSIE authority set with their own current official sources.

Mandatory identifies a requirement directly supported by a current official source for the stated ASE/CSIE route. Conditional identifies an official requirement that applies only when its stated trigger is present. Recommended identifies an authorial risk-control practice from this guide, not an institutional requirement. A recommendation does not become mandatory merely because it appears in the book.

Table A.1 separates official obligations from authorial controls. Each mandatory or conditional entry ends with the controlling source code and verification date. Session dates, room numbers, contact addresses, platform windows, file-naming strings, fees and current form labels are deliberately omitted because they are volatile and belong in the applicable official calendar, page or form.

The table is intentionally stricter about source control than about interface detail. A current faculty page may operationalise a regulation through a platform, form or departmental channel that did not exist when the regulation was issued. That implementation must be followed when it is current and compatible with the governing rule. An apparent conflict is not resolved by memory, a saved message or a previous cohort’s package.

A student should therefore not ask only whether an item is present. The stronger control question is whether the item is required for the correct route, supported by a current authority, identical across all submitted copies where identity is required and backed by evidence that can be produced under examination. Administrative completion without version identity is defective. Scientific quality without completion of the current official route is not eligible for examination.

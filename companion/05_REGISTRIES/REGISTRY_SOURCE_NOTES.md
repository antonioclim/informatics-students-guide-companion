# Registry source notes

**Verification date:** 19 August 2026

The institutional registry contains only official ASE or CSIE sources. Formal regulations and guidance are distinguished from volatile operational pages. The official source must be opened again immediately before use.

The tool registry contains official provider documentation or help pages. It records a bounded capability and a critical limitation. It does not endorse a tool, guarantee availability or treat generated output as evidence.

The tool and institutional registries are maintenance objects. Their rows may be superseded without changing the stable route and gate doctrine of the book.

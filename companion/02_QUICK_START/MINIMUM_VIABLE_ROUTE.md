# Minimum viable route under deadline pressure

1. Name the assessed product and dominant inquiry route.
2. Identify the current gate and write its exact decision question.
3. Name the single evidence object that most constrains the decision.
4. Complete the corresponding minimum record.
5. Obtain a reviewable disposition: pass, pass with conditions, repair, return, await authority or stop.
6. Record consequential AI use in TRACE.
7. Preserve the version and do not overwrite a failed or superseded state.

This route reduces documentation burden. It does not remove evidence, authority, ethics, rights or disclosure obligations.

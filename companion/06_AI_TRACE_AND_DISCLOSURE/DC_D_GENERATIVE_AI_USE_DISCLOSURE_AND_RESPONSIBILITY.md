A controlled intervention record, not a transfer of authorship

Generative AI can accelerate comparison, explanation, drafting, coding and rehearsal, but fluency is not evidence and convenience is not permission. Treat every output as a candidate intervention whose admissibility depends on the applicable rule, the information supplied to the system, the intellectual task retained by the student and the verification performed afterwards. The student remains the author of the work and is responsible for every retained claim, source, method, artefact and disclosure.

Mandatory identifies a current institutional obligation or a non-negotiable integrity boundary. Conditional identifies a use or record activated by permission, data classification, task, risk or route. Recommended identifies an authorial control that improves traceability without claiming independent institutional force. The stricter applicable rule governs: law, institutional guidance, programme or course instructions, examination rules, data-owner terms, supervisor directions and host-organisation policies may each narrow what is permitted.

This module uses consequential use as a conservative TRACE trigger. A use is consequential when it can alter the problem, method, evidence, code, analysis, claim, disclosure or assessed wording. A use may be non-substantial under one external guideline yet still be consequential for this project and therefore worth recording. Conversely, a TRACE record never creates permission and never replaces the official declaration or another required form.

## Permission and decision boundary

Decide before use, not after the output has become embedded in the work. Under the ASE guidance checked for this module, student use is not authorised in the absence of specific instructions. Where an assessed activity, examination, supervisor, data owner or organisation requires explicit authorisation, technical availability is not permission. ## TRACE: the minimum consequential-use record

TRACE is the book’s transverse control for consequential generative-AI intervention. It is a private project record unless a competent rule requires submission or public disclosure. Preserve enough context to reconstruct the decision, but do not copy personal, confidential or restricted content into a public log. The official declaration, manuscript disclosure and TRACE record are linked but distinct.

## Independent verification, not model agreement

A second model can be useful as an adversarial question generator, discrepancy detector or counterexample source. It is not automatically an independent verifier. Models may share training material, retrieval sources, design assumptions, prompt framing and generic failure modes, while repeated use of the same model can create correlated failures. Verification is independent only when the decisive evidence or assessor is independent of the generated wording and capable of falsifying it.

## Disclosure, declaration and authorship

Maintain three linked layers. First, TRACE records the consequential process and disposition. Second, the manuscript, methods section, acknowledgement or artefact documentation explains the material influence in the form required by the discipline, publisher or assessment. Third, the official institutional declaration records use or non-use through the current form and route. No layer silently replaces another.

Under the ASE guidance checked for this module, students and graduates must declare whether generative AI was used. For a final degree work, the declaration is placed immediately after Contents and a separate handwritten-signed copy is submitted at registration. Complete the current official form exactly as required and reverify its placement, signature and submission route; do not rely on a frozen annex number in this book.

Citation and disclosure also serve different functions. Cite the primary source actually read for a substantive claim. Do not cite a generated reference instead of that source. Where the AI interaction itself is the object of study or a material method, preserve and describe it with the tool, model or version where available, date, task, relevant input boundary, affected artefact, output disposition and verification. Follow the required citation style while keeping the official declaration unchanged.

Do not infer that minor language support is exempt from the institutional declaration merely because it may not require a bibliographic entry. Conversely, do not turn the reference list into a substitute for a clear methods or acknowledgement statement. The disclosure must allow a reasonable reader or examiner to understand what the system influenced and how the retained result was checked.

A generative system cannot be an author, co-author, supervisor, reviewer, approver or rights holder because it cannot accept responsibility, resolve conflicts or warrant the integrity of the work. Human authors remain accountable for the complete manuscript and associated artefacts, including content that they accepted after AI assistance.

## Confidentiality, rights and release boundary

Do not enter personal data, participant material, confidential organisational information, unpublished results, licensed databases, private code, security details, access credentials, examination content, peer-review material or another person’s protected work into an external, public or institutionally unvalidated system. Redaction is not automatically sufficient: context, combinations of attributes, metadata and attachments may remain identifying or protected.

Use the minimum necessary input in an environment authorised for the information class and purpose. Check the actual provider, deployment, retention, training and access settings in force. A privacy label, enterprise tier or training opt-out may reduce one risk but does not replace the data owner’s permission, a lawful basis, ethics approval, contractual restrictions or institutional validation.

Generated text, code, images and other artefacts may reproduce protected material or carry incompatible licence, attribution or provenance conditions. Inspect third-party dependencies and notices, retain the rights decision and block public release when ownership or reuse remains unresolved. Never assume that an output is free to publish merely because the interface generated it.

Keep the full TRACE record and sensitive interaction history in the controlled private or restricted workspace. Release only the disclosure and supporting material that is authorised, necessary and safe. Before applying AI to files received from others, inspect for comments, invisible text, metadata, embedded objects or hidden instructions that could expose information or manipulate an automated workflow.

## Defence ownership and stop rules

The final test is oral and operational ownership. The student must be able to explain why the tool was used, what changed, which alternatives were rejected, how every material output was verified and how the substantive result can be reproduced without the original chat. Retained prose does not become independently authored merely because it was lightly edited.

Stop and reopen the responsible gate when a source cannot be resolved, a calculation cannot be reproduced, code cannot be explained or tested, rights remain unclear, a confidential input was exposed, a substantial use was omitted from disclosure or the official declaration no longer matches the work. Resolve the defect before submission. A polished final file is not evidence that an uncontrolled intervention became acceptable.

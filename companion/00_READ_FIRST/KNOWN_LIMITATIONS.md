1. Institutional and tool registries are dated snapshots and can become obsolete after 19 August 2026.
2. The worked examples are synthetic and cannot support empirical claims.
3. CSV files do not enforce validation rules or permissions; users must preserve controlled vocabularies manually or through their own tooling.
4. The workbook has a LibreOffice technical proof only. It is not the final Microsoft Word proof.
5. No strict PDF/UA, WCAG or publisher-specific compliance claim is made.
6. No public repository, DOI, licence, ISBN, CIP, imprint or confirmed edition statement exists.
7. The package does not resolve publisher rights or the publication status of the book, figures or companion.
8. Tool capabilities, model names, plans, regional availability and connected-source permissions are volatile.

**Minimum planning evidence:** Dominant logic, integration point, adequate resources and both evidence contracts.  
**Immediate warning:** Two incomplete studies without integration.

## Hostile defence lens

**Question:** Why is the project not simply three unrelated methods?  
**Bounded answer:** The components answer one integrated question, retain separate evidence obligations and meet only at the predeclared joint display. Without that integration product, the hybrid label would be unjustified.

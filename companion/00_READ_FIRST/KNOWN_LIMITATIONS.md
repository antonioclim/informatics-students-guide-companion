1. Institutional links, calendars and session-specific values are dated records and require re-verification before operational use.
2. Synthetic worked examples demonstrate structure and reasoning but are not empirical findings and must not be represented as real cases.
3. Automated validation and hashes establish package consistency, not scientific reproducibility, legal clearance or currency of external requirements.
4. The version-specific DOI https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22017362 identifies the exact v1.2.0 archive. The all-versions DOI https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22017361 resolves to the latest Zenodo version and may therefore resolve to a later object in the future.
5. No support-service level, ISBN, CIP, imprint or publisher endorsement is asserted by the repository.
